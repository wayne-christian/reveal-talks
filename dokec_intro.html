Connecting the dots

Priorities for the rest of the academic year

My priority is to better connect the expertise, projects and external relationships that already exist across the School. The aim isn't for everyone to do everything, but to create clearer, supported routes into activity.

MAP → ENABLE → GROW

Research ↔ Professional practice

Knowledge exchange ↔ Commercialisation

Three stages: understand what we have, enable people to participate, then grow those connections into sustainable activity.

MAP

Understand what we have

Short School-wide skills and interests questionnaire

Identify current and potential expertise

Understand existing external relationships

Identify areas with potential for KE and commercial activity

Skills + Interest + Capacity

Establish who wants to engage

Identify current levels of capacity

Understand what support colleagues need

Develop a searchable School skills directory

Make it easier to find expertise and potential collaborators

This isn't just about producing a skills directory. I want to understand interest, capacity and support needs, so we can make useful connections rather than simply catalogue expertise.

ENABLE

Connect people and develop capability

Establish a School KE and commercialisation forum

Use Champions to showcase successful projects and good practice

Introduce mentoring for proposals and project development

Invite guest speakers with relevant external experience

Run focused showcases, project retrospectives and open sessions

Enable Participation

Connect research-focused and professional-practice colleagues

Provide practical help with proposals, KTPs and consultancy

Signpost staff to Business Engagement and specialist support

Create routes for experienced colleagues to become Champions and mentors

Participation doesn't mean everyone needs to lead a project. Someone might contribute expertise, join a supported project, then potentially lead activity themselves and eventually mentor others.

GROW

Extend connections into sustainable activity

Match external needs with School expertise

Develop consultancy and commercial opportunities

Grow KE partnerships (KTP and AKT participation)

Strengthen collaborative research

Develop CPD, short courses and educational contracts

What happens next?

1. Complete the questionnaire
Tell us about your expertise, interests, external relationships and capacity.

2. Join the conversation
Share current projects, become a Champion, request mentoring or contribute to an existing opportunity.

3. Help connect the dots
Identify where research, professional practice and external engagement could work together.

Connect the dots

The aim is not to ask everyone to do everything.
It is to understand our strengths, make the right connections and create supported routes into activity.
